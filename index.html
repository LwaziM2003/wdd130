Swaziland

At RapidRush Rafting, our mission is to deliver unforgettable river experiences with the highest safety standards, passionate guides, and a love for nature. Join us as we conquer the rapids and build memories!

Swaziland, now known as Eswatini, is a small landlocked country in Southern Africa. It is known for its rich culture, traditional ceremonies, and beautiful landscapes.

Eswatini is bordered by South Africa to the west, south, and southeast, and Mozambique to the northeast.

The country is famous for its vibrant markets, wildlife reserves, and the annual Umhlanga Reed Dance, a cultural event that celebrates the Swazi culture and attracts thousands of visitors each year.
